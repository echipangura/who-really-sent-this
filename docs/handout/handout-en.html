  2. Copy the email's hidden lines (headers).

  3. Paste them in the box and click Check this email.
✅ Checks outReally from that address. If it asks for money or passwords, still check another way.
⚠️ Be carefulSomething is wrong. Check with the sender another way first.
⛔ Likely a scamNot from who it says. Do not click, reply or pay.

5 warning signs

  • Hurry or fear: “Your account closes today.”
  • Asks for money, gift cards, passwords or codes.
  • Name and address don't match: “Chase Bank” from a Gmail address.
  • Look-alike address: paypa1.com, amaz0n-support.net
  • Replies go somewhere else than the sender.

If it looks like a scam

  • Do not click links or call numbers in the email.
  • Go to the real website yourself, or call a number you trust (on your card or bill).
  • Never send passwords, codes or bank details by email.
  • Report it: reportfraud.ftc.gov and “Report phishing” in your mail app.
🔒 Nothing you paste is saved or sent anywhere. The tool never asks for a password.
A ✅ means the owner of the address sent it, not that it is safe. A hacked account still passes.
